Understanding Trauma Bonds

TraumaBond is an awareness platform designed to help people understand unhealthy emotional attachment, recognize warning signs, and find supportive resources.

WHAT IS IT?

Trauma Bonding

A trauma bond can develop when a person becomes emotionally attached to someone who repeatedly hurts, manipulates, or mistreats them while also providing periods of affection, reassurance, or kindness.

🔄

The Cycle

Harmful behaviour may be followed by affection, apologies, or calm periods, creating a repeating cycle.

💭

Emotional Attachment

Strong emotions can make it difficult to recognize unhealthy patterns or leave the relationship.

🌱

Awareness

Learning about these patterns can help someone understand their experiences and seek support.

LEARN

Recognizing the Warning Signs

01

Repeated Hurt

Someone repeatedly hurts, insults, controls, threatens, or manipulates you.

02

Extreme Highs and Lows

The relationship alternates between painful experiences and intense affection.

03

Feeling Responsible

You feel responsible for the other person's behaviour, emotions, or happiness.

04

Difficulty Leaving

Even when you recognize the relationship is harmful, leaving feels extremely difficult.

05

Isolation

You become increasingly disconnected from friends, family, or other sources of support.

06

Walking on Eggshells

You constantly worry about triggering anger, conflict, or punishment.

SUPPORT

You Don't Have to Handle Everything Alone

Reaching out for support can be difficult, but you deserve relationships where you feel safe, respected, and heard.

Talk to Someone

Consider speaking with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, counsellor, or mental-health professional.

Prioritize Safety

If someone is threatening or hurting you, prioritize your immediate safety and contact appropriate local emergency or support services.

Be Kind to Yourself

Experiencing difficulty leaving an unhealthy relationship does not make you weak.
